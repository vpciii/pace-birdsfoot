Saved carts today

    Enter a cart number and tap a hole to save that cart (with its note, if any); tap a saved cart (or type its number) to bring it back. Pace is measured at the moment you tap the hole. Saved carts are kept until you clear them or the day ends. Checkpoints count from the start hole: 2nd hole played → 0:25 · 5th → 1:15 · 7th → 1:40 · 9th → 2:10 · 13th → 3:15 · 15th → 3:40 · 18th → 4:20. Other holes are interpolated by par, so a par 5 gets more time than a par 3. For a shotgun, set Start hole to where the group teed off; B groups get one hole's pace (14 min) added at every checkpoint. Behind = orange (red at 15+ min), ahead = green; updates every 30 s.
